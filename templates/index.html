OUR STORY

Two sides,
one story

as told by Anna

How I Met Your Father

I remember the first time I saw Victor. It was at a mutual friend's wedding in Port Harcourt. He was standing by the corner, laughing with a group of friends. He wasn't the loudest person in the room, but he had this quiet confidence that drew me in immediately.

"Sometimes, God writes the most beautiful stories in the most unexpected ways."

We started talking later that evening. I found myself completely captivated by his depth, his vision, and his kindness. We talked for hours about everything—from our childhood dreams to the kind of future we wanted to build. I remember going home that night and thinking, “Wow, this person is so special.”

Fast forward a few months, and we found ourselves planning what I thought was just a weekend getaway. He was acting so nervous, checking his pocket constantly. I didn't realize until we were walking along the beach at sunset that he had been carrying a ring around all day.

When he got down on one knee, time literally stopped. The sound of the waves, the soft sand... it was perfect. And I said yes!

How I Met Your Mother

I wasn't really looking for love when I met Anna. I was focused on my career and just trying to navigate life. But the moment I saw her at that wedding, everything changed. She had this incredible smile that lit up the entire room.

"It wasn't just that I liked her. I knew I could build a life with her."

I remember building up the courage to walk over and say hello. We ended up talking for hours. What struck me most was her heart. She was intentional, thoughtful, and so incredibly present in our conversation. I knew right away that she was different.

Planning the proposal was the hardest secret I've ever kept. I wanted it to be perfect. I spent weeks coordinating with her friends and family to make sure everything was aligned. When we went for that walk on the beach, my heart was pounding out of my chest.

When she looked at me and said yes, it was the most overwhelming feeling of joy I have ever experienced. I am so incredibly grateful to God for bringing her into my life. I am ready for forever with her.
